# Standalone hardware architecture sources

[Research links and claim-level qualifications](../../raw/standalone-hardware-architecture-2026-09-29.md), recorded 2026-09-29. No hardware probe took place.

Third-party RX3 firmware research reports i.MX 6Quad, ARMv7-A, and Linux 3.0.101. Service documents identify i.MX6 processors in the XZ and RR. The RR document gives an i.MX6 Dual part number. The AZ open-source distribution supports only a narrower claim: Linux-related components exist. It does not identify the AZ processor.

Third-party PRIME 4 firmware research reports armv7l Linux. This report does not establish the architecture of other Denon units. None of these sources measures free RAM or demonstrates a supported plugin or internal audio/control interface.
